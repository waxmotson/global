        How to play

        Somewhere on Earth there is a mystery first-level subdivision — a state, province, region, prefecture, canton, emirate… There are thousands of them. Find it.

        1. Type any subdivision and pick it from the list.
        2. It lights up on the globe. Cold blue means far away, hot red means close. Distance is measured border to border, so a neighbour is 0 km away.
        3. Keep narrowing in until you hit the mystery region.

        Drag to spin the globe, scroll or pinch to zoom. Stuck? Hints reveal the kind of unit, then its country, then its first letter.

        Daily is the same region for everyone each day. Practice is unlimited.

        Boundaries: Natural Earth (public domain). A few countries are grouped up to their true first level (e.g. French regions, Italian regions, UK countries).

        Practice countries

        Tick the countries the mystery region may come from. Suggestions are limited to them too. Leave everything unticked to play the whole world.
